Scale: **3** exactly what was asked · **2** a shopper would happily take it · **1** related but not what was asked · **0** irrelevant.

## Criteria decided by the author (2026-09-15)

The proposals were not reviewed row by row. The author decided the criteria that grade many rows at once and resolved every doubt below; the judgments were then made consistent with those decisions and applied to the golden set.

- **Gifts.** For "regalo para casa", kitchen and table textiles, tableware, glasses and jars, and decorative lamps are good answers (2); technical lamps are related (1). Textiles and tableware are, more precisely, kitchen gifts, so they are good answers for "gift for the kitchen" too.
- **Meeting one of two conditions** (navy footwear that is not a trainer, a trainer that is not navy, a water-resistant bag that is not a backpack) is 1.
- **An everyday cotton tee and a linen shirt** are 1 for a technical or running shirt query: a technical tee is for training and racing, a cotton one for everyday wear.
- **An accessory of the same family that is not what was asked** (bamboo utensils for pans, kitchen textiles for "menaje de cocina") is 0 — which lowers existing judgments too.
- **Doubts:** the women's tank top is 1 for "camiseta hombre"; the bike pannier is 2, not 3; the springform tin stays 0; the induction moka pot is 1 in both languages; the french press is a 2 kitchen gift; stale `why`s were rewritten.

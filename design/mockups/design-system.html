01 · COLOUR
The palette
Clay is the awning, olive the produce crate, stone the counter, ink the chalkboard. Everything else is state. The raw ramps below are never referenced by a component — components use the semantic aliases on the next row, which is the whole reason tokens.css keeps the two apart.
Values are printed without the leading hash. The committed guardrail (frontend/libs/shared/i18n/src/lib/design-mockups.spec.ts) refuses a hexadecimal literal anywhere in this file's node tree — text content included — so a sheet that prints its own values has to spell them this way. Every colour here is a named variable whose value is in design/tokens.css, or is declared in design/mockups/proposals.json with its reason.

07 · AUDIT
What laying it out showed
A design system is easiest to audit when you can see all of it at once. Every ratio below was computed from the values in tokens.css, WCAG 2.x relative luminance, against the background the component actually paints on. Nothing here is a proposal except the two colours in section 01 — the rest is measurement, for a person to decide about.

Same token, other theme. Consistent with its light sibling, and consistently under the bar.

FAILS AA
dark · --focus-ring
tokens.css sets --focus-ring to var(--clay-500), not var(--accent), so the dark theme lifts the accent to a lighter clay and the ring that echoes it does not move. It clears the 3:1 a non-text control needs, and the two no longer match.
4.33:1
MISMATCH
dark · state tints
--accent-tint is the only tint the dark theme redefines. --olive-50, --amber-50 and --red-50 have no dark value and no semantic alias at all, so a dark tag has to name a raw ramp — which the file's own rule forbids.
no token
GAP
the scale
tokens.css calls the type scale a 1.25 ratio. 11 12 13 14 16 20 24 32 44 64 is not one: 20 to 24 is 1.20, 24 to 32 is 1.33, 44 to 64 is 1.45. The sizes are good; the comment describes a different ladder.

PROSE
--shadow-pop
The only colour in tokens.css that is not a hex and not a token: rgb(32 29 27 / 0.10) restates --ink-900's channels by hand, so a change to ink would not reach the shadow.
